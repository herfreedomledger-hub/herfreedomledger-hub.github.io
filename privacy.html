Privacy Policy

We collect as little as we can get away with, we never sell it, and your budget numbers never touch our servers. Here is exactly what that means.

Last updated: October 2026

The most important part first

The Freedom Budget Kit is an Excel file that runs entirely on your computer. The numbers you type into your workbook are never sent to us or anyone else. We can't see your salary, your bills, your debts or your savings. Ever.

What we do collect

  • Checkout details — your name, email address, and payment information, collected by our payment provider (a Merchant of Record) to process your order. We see your name, email and what you ordered — not your card details.
  • Email you send us — if you write to support@herfreedomledger.com, we keep that correspondence to answer you.
  • Basic website analytics — if any: anonymous page-view counts. We don't run advertising trackers or sell traffic data.

Why we have it

To deliver your order, reply to support requests within 48 hours, and keep records the tax office requires. That's the full list of purposes.

Who else sees it

  • Our payment provider, to process checkout and refunds (their own privacy terms apply to their systems).
  • Email delivery services, to send you the files and receipts.
  • Accounting and legal obligations, where the law in our jurisdiction requires record-keeping.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing, ever.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax law requires; support emails while the conversation stays useful to you; then deleted.

Your rights

Where your local law gives you rights over your data (access, correction, deletion, export), you have them with us. To use any of them, email support@herfreedomledger.com — same address, same 48-hour reply promise.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party ad pixels. If we ever add analytics, this page will say exactly which tool and what it counts.

Children

Her Freedom Ledger is sold to adults budgeting their own money. We don't collect data from children, and the kit isn't designed for users under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: support@herfreedomledger.com — replies within 48 hours.
